1. Introduction

Slow Software Studio ("we", "our", or "us") operates GeoSentinel ("the App"), a mobile application providing real-time 3D geospatial intelligence, offline map rendering, satellite orbit tracking, and tactical situational awareness.

GeoSentinel is completely free to download and use. We do not offer paid tiers, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. The application is supported solely through advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

This Privacy Policy explains how GeoSentinel handles user data, permissions, device identifiers, and third-party advertising in strict compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies and Google AdMob Publisher Policies.

2. Permissions and Information Processed

A. Precise & Approximate Location Data

  • Permissions Requested: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION.
  • Usage: When explicitly granted by you, location data is used strictly in the foreground to center the 3D globe and tactical HUD on your position, calculate relative line-of-sight to overhead satellite passes, and display local coordinates.
  • Storage and Transmission: We do NOT store, log, track, sell, or transmit your GPS coordinates to any server. Location calculation takes place entirely on your device in active memory and is cleared upon exiting the application. GeoSentinel does not run background location services.

B. Device and Network State

  • Permissions Requested: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE.
  • Usage: Used to check whether your device has an active Wi-Fi or cellular connection to stream live telemetry (satellite orbits, maritime AIS, seismic feeds) and load online map tiles. When offline, GeoSentinel switches seamlessly to local cached vector assets.

C. Local App Preferences & Caching

  • Storage: User settings (selected map layers, custom visual modes, and offline cached tiles) are stored locally on your device via standard Android Shared Preferences and sandboxed app storage.
  • This data is private to your device and is never uploaded to external developer servers.

D. No Financial or Payment Information Collected

  • GeoSentinel does not feature in-app purchases, digital unlocks, memberships, or paid subscriptions.
  • We do not collect, request, access, or store credit card numbers, bank accounts, or financial transaction details of any kind.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob Disclosures

To keep GeoSentinel 100% free for all users without subscription paywalls, the App displays in-app advertisements powered by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

A. Data Processed by Google AdMob

The Google Mobile Ads SDK integrated into GeoSentinel may collect, process, and share certain information automatically, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) via com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, unique device identifiers, and mobile operating system version.
  • Location Information: General location (such as country, city, or IP-derived coarse area) used to serve geographically relevant ads.
  • Ad & App Interaction Data: Ad views, impression counts, clicks, video interactions, and diagnostic metrics (such as crash logs and ad load latency) for fraud prevention, billing, and system stability.
  • IP Address: Used for diagnostic purposes, fraud detection, and general geographic estimation.

B. How Google Uses This Information

For complete details on how Google processes data when you use partner apps, please review Google's official disclosure:

You may also review Google's Advertising Technologies Policy and Google's General Privacy Policy.

C. User Choice and Opting Out of Personalized Ads

  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: On Android devices (Android 12+), you can reset or delete your Advertising ID by going to:
    Settings > Privacy > Ads or Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Opt Out of Personalized Ads: On earlier Android versions, enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization". When disabled, you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests or activity.

D. European Union / UK Consent (GDPR)

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, we implement Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) / certified Consent Management Platform (CMP). Where required, you are presented with a consent banner allowing you to accept or manage preferences regarding cookies, mobile identifiers, and personalized vs. non-personalized advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

The App connects only to the following authorized third-party services:

  • Google Play Services: Core application delivery, stability updates, and runtime infrastructure. (Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: Monetization and in-app advertisement delivery. (AdMob Terms & Technologies)
  • Authoritative Public Feeds: Public open-source endpoints for live satellite ephemeris, maritime transponders, and seismic events (e.g., USGS, OpenSky). Requests to these public APIs contain no personal data or device identifiers.

6. Data Security and Transmission

All network communications between GeoSentinel, public telemetry sources, and Google services are encrypted in transit using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS) protocols. We do not operate external developer servers that store your personal records.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Because GeoSentinel does not require accounts or store personal data on remote servers, we retain no personal records.
  • All locally stored preferences, search histories, and cached map tiles can be permanently removed at any time by clearing application data via your device's Settings > Apps > GeoSentinel > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.
