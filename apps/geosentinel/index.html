1. Introduction

Slow Software Studio ("we", "our", or "us") operates GeoSentinel ("the App"), an interactive 3D planetary visualization and navigation platform featuring real-time flight paths, maritime vessel tracking, satellite orbits, seismic alerts, and offline map navigation.

GeoSentinel is completely free to use with no subscriptions, paid tiers, or in-app purchases. The application is supported by advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

This Privacy Policy explains how our App handles permissions, location data, and advertising identifiers in accordance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies and Google AdMob Publisher Policies.

2. Permissions and Information Handled

A. Location Information

  • Permissions Requested: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION.
  • Usage: When granted by you, location is used strictly in the foreground to center the 3D globe and maps on your current location and calculate relative distances to overhead satellites or navigational coordinates.
  • Local Processing: We do not track, log, store, sell, or transmit your location data to any server. Location data is processed locally in active memory on your device and discarded when the session ends. GeoSentinel never runs background location tracking.

B. Network State

  • Permissions Requested: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE.
  • Usage: Used solely to check for an active internet connection to download online map tiles and public live streams (such as flights and weather). When offline, the app operates completely from local offline cached maps.

C. Local Device Storage

  • User settings (such as layer choices, display modes, and downloaded map packages) are saved strictly on your local device storage.
  • No personal data is uploaded to external developer servers.

D. No Payment or Financial Data Collected

  • GeoSentinel is completely free. We do not process, collect, or store credit cards, bank accounts, or financial transaction details.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob

To support ongoing maintenance and provide free access to all users, GeoSentinel displays in-app advertisements through Google AdMob (Google LLC).

A. Data Processed by Google AdMob

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process certain technical data to deliver and measure advertisements, including:

  • Device Identifiers: The Google Advertising ID (AAID), general device identifiers, and OS version.
  • Coarse Location: General location (such as country or city derived from IP address) to deliver geographically appropriate ads.
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Ad impressions, click data, and diagnostic performance logs used for fraud prevention and stability.
  • IP Address: Used for general location estimation and platform security.

B. How Google Uses Information

For full details on Google's advertising data handling, please consult Google's official partner disclosure:
👉 How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You may also review Google's Advertising Technologies Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

C. User Controls & Opt-Out

  • Manage or Delete Advertising ID: On Android devices (Android 12+), you can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time via:
    Settings > Privacy > Ads or Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Opt Out of Personalized Ads: On earlier Android versions, enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization". When enabled, ads remain visible but are not tailored to your interests.

D. European & UK Consent (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, we use Google's certified Consent Management Platform (CMP). When required, you will see a consent prompt where you can manage your advertising choices.

4. Third-Party Services

GeoSentinel connects to the following authorized third-party platforms:

  • Google Play Services: Used for application delivery, security updates, and core runtime support. (Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: In-app ad monetization. (AdMob Terms)
  • Public Telemetry & Map Servers: Public endpoints providing open data feeds (such as OpenSky, USGS, OpenStreetMap). These requests contain no personal identifiers.

6. Data Security and Deletion

  • All data transmissions between the App and public data endpoints use industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS) encryption.
  • Because GeoSentinel does not maintain user accounts, no personal profile data is stored on remote servers.
  • You can clear all locally cached maps and preferences at any time by clearing the App's storage via Settings > Apps > GeoSentinel > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.

7. Children's Privacy

GeoSentinel is intended for a general audience and is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at help.slowsoftware@gmail.com.
